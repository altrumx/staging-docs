## Navigating the Dashboard

Once logged in, you'll be directed to the **Home Page**, the control centre of your AltrumAI experience.

### The Home Page Includes:
- **Navigation Panel** — Quick access to Home, Organisation, AI Systems, Violation Logs, Manage Users, Support, and Documentation.
- **Summary Cards** — Key metrics at a glance: Active AI Systems, Active Systems without Guardrails, Violation Rate, and Total Cost.
- **Top 5 AI Systems by Usage and Cost** — A ranked view of your most active AI systems by token usage and cost over the selected date range.
- **Top 5 AI Systems by Messages and Violations** — A ranked view showing messages scanned and violation counts per AI system.
- **Most Frequent Violations** — A breakdown of the most common violation types across all AI systems, ranked by volume, showing blocked, masked, and monitored counts per violation.

## Creating and Configuring a New AI System

### Step 1: Launch the AI System Wizard

1. Click the **Add AI System** button in the left-hand navigation panel
2. A popup will appear for you to enter your AI system details.

<img height="200" src="/images/ai-systems/add-ai-system-button.png" />

---

### Step 2: Enter AI System Details

1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **AI System Name** (required)
   - **AI System Description** (required)
   - **AI System Purpose** (required)
   - **AI System Owner** (required)
   - **AI Providers** (select one or more)

Click **"Create"** to save.

> **Note:** Your AI system will only function with the AI providers you select during setup. Ensure you choose all providers you plan to use with this system.

**Setting Up Your AI System:** Once created, your AI system is in **Inactive** status. We recommend completing the following steps before activating.

### **Step 3: Assess your AI System (Optional)**

Running an assessment is not required but recommended. It identifies the risks specific to your system and pre-configures the most relevant guardrails for you.

1. On the AI System Details tab, click **Start Assessment**.
2. The assessment agent will guide you through a short conversation about your system. Most assessments take less than 10 minutes.
3. Once complete, your identified risks, recommended controls, and suggested guardrails will be displayed on the Assessment Results screen.

> If you already know which guardrails apply to your system, you can skip this step and configure them directly (from System Details Tab). You can always come back and run an assessment later.

### **Step 4: Configure Guardrails**

1. If you completed an assessment, click **Review & Test Guardrails** on the Assessment Results screen. If you skipped the assessment, navigate to your AI System "System Detail" Tab and click **Configure Guardrails**.
2. Review and configure each guardrail using the right-hand panel:
   - Enable or disable the guardrail.
   - Set enforcement mode (Monitor, Block, or Mask).
   - Adjust thresholds or select relevant criteria (topics, entities, words and phrases).
3. Test your configuration in the built-in sandbox environment:

   - Interact with a test chatbot.
   - Validate how guardrails behave in real time.
   - Review flagged inputs and outputs.
4. Once satisfied, click **Continue to Activate AI System**.

> **Note:**  
> For more detail on individual guardrails, refer to the [Guardrail Configuration Guide](/guardrails).

### **Step 5: Activate your AI System**

1. On the AI System Details, click **Activate AI System** in ellipses 
2. A confirmation popup will appear. Click **Activate** to confirm.
3. Your AI system status will update to **Active** immediately.
4. A success notification will confirm activation.

> **Note:**<br />Guardrails configuration is not mandatory for AI System Activation, in cases when Organisation just need to use AI Gateway

### Step 4: Review and Activate AI System

1. Review your project’s setup on the **Summary Page**.
2. Use the edit icons to make changes to any step if needed.
3. Once confirmed, click **Activate AI System**.
4. A success message will appear
5. Click **View AI System** to open the **AI System Dashboard**.

## AI System Dashboard

Once an AI system is published, the **AI System Dashboard** becomes the central view for all configuration, activity, and guardrail enforcement related to that AI system.

Key sections include:
- **AI System Details**
- **Guardrail Summary**
- **API Key Access**
- **Usage and Violation Metrics**

<img height="200" src="/images/ai-systems/system-dashboard.png" />

## Editing AI System Details

To Edit an Existing AI System:

1. Navigate to the **Home Page**.
2. In the **AI Systems Table**, find your AI system and click the **View** in the last column.
3. On the **AI System Dashboard**, Click **Edit** to return to **Step 1** of the setup process
4. Modify the necessary fields and click **Save Changes**.
