## Overview-

The **Guardrail Configuration** module enables Organisation Owners and Admins to define and manage guardrails for their AI systems. You can configure guardrails in three ways:

- After completing an AI system assessment — risks are identified and guardrail recommendations are suggested.
- Independently at any time — without taking an assessment, whether your AI system is Active or Inactive.
- To update an existing configuration — revisit and edit guardrails you have already set up.

---

## **Accessing Guardrail Configuration**

From your AI System System Details page, click **Configure Guardrails** if no guardrails are set up yet, or **Manage Guardrails** if you already have an existing configuration.

If you have just completed an assessment, click **Review & Test Guardrails** on the Assessment Results screen.

## **Step 1: Select a Guardrail**

Click on a guardrail from the list on the left. Its configuration options will appear in the right-hand panel.

---

## **Step 2: Configure the Guardrail**

Each guardrail has its own configuration options. Common settings include:

- **Enable the Guardrail** using the toggle at the top of the panel.
- Choose an **Enforcement Mode** — Input, Output, or Both.
- Select a **Guardrail Behaviour**:
  - **Monitor** — Observe without taking action.
  - **Block** — Stop content that violates the guardrail.
  - **Mask** — Replace sensitive information with anonymised placeholders (Data Protection guardrails only).
- Adjust **Thresholds** and configure relevant topics, entities, categories, or words and phrases depending on the guardrail type.

> For more detail on each guardrail type, refer to the Guardrails section of the documentation.

---

## **Step 3: Testing Enabled Guardrails**

Click **Save & Continue** to proceed to the Test Configuration screen.

The Test Configuration screen is a sandbox environment where you can:

- Interact with a test chatbot.
- See your configured guardrails in action.
- Observe how inputs and outputs are Masked, flagged and blocked in real time.

If you need to make changes based on your testing, click **Back** to return to the Guardrail Configuration screen.

> Testing is optional but recommended before applying changes to a live AI system.

---

## **Applying Guardrails**

When satisfied with your testing, click the primary CTA at the bottom of the Test Configuration screen:

- **Complete Assessment** — if you arrived via an assessment. Returns you to AI System Overview with the assessment marked Completed and guardrails applied.
- **Apply Guardrails** — if you are configuring or updating independently.

> If your AI system is **Active**, guardrail changes take effect immediately. If your AI system is **Inactive**, guardrails will be applied when you activate it.

---

## **Editing or Disabling Guardrails**

1. From the AI System Overview page, click **Manage Guardrails**.
2. Enable or disable guardrails and make any configuration changes.
3. Click **Save & Continue** then **Apply Guardrails** to confirm.

---

**Best Practices**

- Start with **Monitor** mode to observe behaviour without disruption.
- Use the **Test Guardrails** sandbox to validate before applying changes.
- Regularly review violation logs and refine configurations as needed.
- Apply stricter enforcement to regulated or high-risk AI [systems.Best](http://systems.Best) Practices
